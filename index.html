01 · Why it works

  1. Ad spend that lifts your organic rank

    Amazon ranks products based on sales velocity, and 80% of sales happen on page one of search results. So we use ad spend to push your ranking: finding high-converting keywords that scale with paid spend, then constantly monitoring, tweaking and testing to drive visibility which leads to more incremental sales.

    Most ad partners lean on branded terms that post high ROAS or put paid spend behind already highly ranked terms which doesn't grow market share. We cut the waste and focus spend on realistic opportunities that actually grow your brand on Amazon.
